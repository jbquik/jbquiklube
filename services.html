Specialty & High‑Capacity Vehicles

Some vehicles need more oil or special canister‑style filters, which adds to the total service cost.

  • High‑performance cars that use up to 10 qts of Mobil 1 full synthetic
  • Diesel cars and trucks that can require 15 qts and specific filters
  • Specialty oils like Amsoil, Royal Purple, Ravenol available by request

Individual quarts and specialty filters are priced separately and will be reviewed with you before authorization.
